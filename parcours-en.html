My life and studies in France

When the war began in Ukraine, my family came to France. I continued my education in the French school system, first in secondary school and then in high school.

In 2023, when choosing my higher education path, I wanted to move towards computer science and cybersecurity. After discovering IUT Nice Côte d'Azur in Sophia Antipolis during an open day, I chose the Bachelor's degree in Networking & Telecommunications.

2023

2023 - 2026

Bachelor's Degree in Networking & Telecommunications

From 2023 to 2026, I completed a three-year Bachelor's degree in Networking & Telecommunications at IUT Nice Côte d'Azur, on the Sophia Antipolis campus.

During these three years, I developed skills in networking, systems administration, cybersecurity, virtualisation, monitoring, automation and DevOps.

In my second year, I completed an 11-week internship at the Inria Centre at Université Côte d'Azur within the DIANA research team. I worked on SLICES-RI, including network experiments on FABRIC, GitLab CI/CD and the automation of VLAN configuration generation.

In my third year, I continued at Inria as an apprentice. I worked on automating the management of SLICES-RI experimental resources with POS and MRS, as well as evaluating the performance of an NVIDIA H100 NVL GPU.

I completed my degree in 2026 with a solid technical foundation and a confirmed interest in networking, systems, cybersecurity and DevOps.
